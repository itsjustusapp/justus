01Who we are

"Us", "we" and "our" refer to the team behind the Us mobile app for Android and iOS and this website. Us is a private space for couples: a day counter, daily questions, goals and date ideas, a private chat, a shared memory timeline and a long distance mode.

This policy applies to the app and this website. When we say "your partner", we mean the person you pair with using a pairing code.

02What we collect

We only collect information the app needs to work. Here is everything, grouped by type.

TypeWhat it includes
AccountAn account ID created automatically when you first open the app. If you choose to save your account, your email address and a password (we store a secure hash, never the password itself).
PairingThe pairing code you create or enter, and which couple space you belong to.
ProfileNicknames, a profile photo if you add one, your relationship start date, important dates, your city and time zone if you turn on long distance mode, and your app preferences.
Your contentChat messages, photos and voice notes you send, hugs, memories with their photos and captions, goals, date ideas, and your answers to daily questions.
ActivityWhich days each of you opened the app, so we can show your shared streak.
DeviceA push notification token, so we can let you know when your partner sends something. You can turn notifications off at any time.

Some settings, such as your partner's city and your chat colours, are stored only on your phone and never leave it.

We do not use advertising SDKs, third party analytics or tracking pixels in the app, and we don't collect your contacts, precise location or browsing history.

03How we use it

  • To create your account and connect you with your partner.
  • To show your shared space: your day counter, milestones, questions, goals, chat, memories and streak.
  • To deliver messages, photos, voice notes and hugs to your partner in real time.
  • To send notifications you've allowed, such as a new message or your daily question.
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems and respond when you contact us.

We never use your conversations, photos or answers for advertising, and we never sell or rent your information to anyone.

04Who can see it

Your partner

Everything you add to your couple space (messages, photos, voice notes, memories, goals, date ideas, answers and hugs) is shared with your partner. That's the point of Us. Your partner can also see your nickname, profile photo, and whether you've opened the app today for your streak.

Nobody else

We don't share your information with other users, advertisers or data brokers. We only share it with the service providers listed below, who help us run the app, or when the law requires us to (for example, a valid court order).

05Service providers

We use a small number of trusted providers to run Us. They process data on our behalf and only for the purposes below.

ProviderWhat they do for us
SupabaseHosts our database, user accounts and file storage (photos, voice notes), and delivers chat in real time.
Google Firebase Cloud MessagingDelivers push notifications to your device. It receives your device token and the notification content.
Apple and GoogleDistribute the app through the App Store and Google Play under their own privacy policies.

Our providers may store data in data centres outside your country. When that happens, we rely on the safeguards they provide, such as standard contractual clauses.

06Storage and security

Your data is encrypted in transit between your phone and our servers, and stored with access rules so that only members of a couple can read that couple's data.

Photos, voice notes and profile pictures are kept in private storage. The app opens them through short-lived links that are only issued to a signed-in member of your couple, so nobody else can open them, even if they somehow get a file's address.

No system is perfectly secure. If we ever learn of a breach that affects your information, we'll tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

07How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account exists, so your shared story is there when you come back to it. If you use disappearing messages, those messages are deleted automatically on our servers after the time you choose.

When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your personal information within 30 days, except where we have to keep something for legal reasons. Backups are cleared on a rolling basis after that.

08Unpairing and deletion

Unpairing

You can unpair at any time from Settings. Unpairing removes you from the shared space with your partner. It doesn't delete your account, and you can pair again later.

Deleting your account

To delete your account and the information connected to it, email us at the address in the Contact section below and include your nickname so we can find your account. Content you've already shared with your partner, such as messages they've received, may remain visible to them until the shared space is deleted.

Signing out

If you haven't saved your account with an email, signing out means you can't get back into that account. The app warns you before this happens.

09Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you and get a copy of it.
  • Correct information that's wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete your information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Receive your information in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent, for example by turning off notifications.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, contact us. We'll reply within 30 days and may need to confirm it's really you first.

10Children

Us is made for adults in relationships and isn't intended for anyone under 16. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

11Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If the change is significant, we'll also let you know in the app before it takes effect.
