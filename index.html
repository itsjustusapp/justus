01

Every day counts.

Us counts from the day your story began and quietly marks the milestones on the way. First week, first month, every anniversary after. A streak keeps you both showing up.

02

One question a day.

350 questions, one at a time. You each pick an answer, then see what the other chose. The little things you didn't know you didn't know. Try it on the phone.

03

Small steps, side by side.

Write down what you're building together and tick it off as you go. Stuck on Saturday? 61 ready made date ideas across 11 moods are one tap away.

04

A chat that's only yours.

Messages, photos and voice notes between just the two of you, in colours you choose. When words are too much effort, send a hug instead.

05

The story so far, kept safe.

Add a memory with a photo and a few words. They gather into a timeline you can scroll back through on the days you need it most.
